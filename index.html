Questions

Before you wake.

What is WORLDS: Anthropos?

A hand-painted, top-down action-adventure RPG. You play Maya, an engineer who wakes on a hostile world after her Ark goes down, with a sword, a sidearm, and one meter that is her ammunition, her healing and her life.

When does it release, and where?

12 November 2027, on Steam for Windows PC. You can wishlist it now, and Steam will tell you the day it launches.

Is there a demo?

A demo is planned for Steam Next Fest in October 2027.

How long is it?

Roughly three to eight hours, depending on how closely you look. There are three endings, your choices decide which one you reach, and New Game Plus waits after.

Does it support controllers?

Not yet. WORLDS: Anthropos is played with keyboard and mouse.

Is it single-player?

Yes. The whole story is played alone.

What are the seven worlds?

WORLDS is a series of seven games. Each one is a complete, standalone game on its own world. None of them is DLC or a withheld chapter. Anthropos is the first.

Who makes it?

Pleroma Studios, an independent studio in New Mexico. Meet the studio.
