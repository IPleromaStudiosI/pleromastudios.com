The world

A green world.
A burning pod.

The year is 2230. Seven Arks left a dying Earth. One of them came down here.

The engineer

Maya

Engineering specialist, second wave. Trained young in judo, karate and kendo, so her hands remember the sword before she does. She wakes in a cherry grove beside her own burning pod, with no memory of the fall.

Her suit converts what she gathers into the energy that keeps her alive. Her weapons draw on the same reserve.

“Every shot’s coming out of me. Make them count.”Maya · voiced by Autumn Teague
World one

Anthropos

A forest world, loud with insects and water. Heket swarm in the wreckage. Lycaon move between the trunks. Something enormous hit the ground ahead of her and left a scar across the green.

Where the cherry trees grow, she can rest, and the world will keep her place.

“Don’t know why I feel safe here. I’ll take it.”Maya
The ship

Ark 7 Malachi

One of seven interstellar vessels sent from a collapsing Earth. Sixteen thousand colonists in cryostasis. A one-way mission, with no return protocols.

The shuttles were ejected before it came down. They were supposed to wake up after it landed.
