Ark 7 Malachi: Mission Statement

Classification: Emigration Directive - Tier One

Authorization: United Earth Coalition [UEC-743-Zeta]

The Ark Initiative represents humanity’s final, unified attempt to ensure survival beyond Earth. Accelerated biospheric collapse beginning in the late 2020s initiated an emergency off-world colonization protocol. Ark 7 - designated Malachi - is one of seven interstellar vessels deployed to candidate exoplanets identified for potential habitation.

This is a one-way mission. No return protocols. No fallback contingencies. Each Ark carries the genetic, intellectual, and cultural seed of our species.

Our objective: establish permanent settlement, ensure multi-generational survival, and transmit findings to Earth until contact is lost or deemed obsolete.

Survival is not guaranteed. Success is imperative.
Godspeed to the crew of Ark 7.
Godspeed to us all.
